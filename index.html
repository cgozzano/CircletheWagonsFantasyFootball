The Constitution

Est. 2013 · Drafted 8/26/2024 · Amended 8/6/2025 · Amended 8/7/2026

Article I — Recognition of One Union

All legislative, judicial, and executive powers are vested in the Supreme Commissioner of Fantasy Football Operations, Chris Gozzano. The Circle the Wagons Fantasy Football League has operated as one union since 2013.

Two Leagues, One Union

Since 2024, the league has operated as two distinct-but-equal divisions of eight members each: the Premier League and the Non-Premier League. Each division runs its own draft, position allocation, point system, and playoff structure — independently agreed to by its own participating members.

Promotion & Relegation

At the close of each season, standings determine movement between divisions:

  • The top 4 finishers of the Non-Premier League (per playoff final standings) are promoted to the Premier League for the following year.
  • The bottom 4 finishers of the Premier League are relegated to the Non-Premier League for the following year.

Amended 8/7/2026, by order of the Supreme Commissioner — the original rule promoted/relegated only the top/bottom 2 teams; it now applies to the top/bottom 4.

Contributions & Payouts

The league-wide minimum contribution is $50 USD per member. Lowering the fee requires unanimous (100%) agreement across all participating members; raising it is negotiated within each division.

To honor the standard of play in the top flight, 25% of all Non-Premier League contributions is remitted to the Premier League's payout pot — the remaining 75% stays within the Non-Premier League, distributed per that division's own agreed structure.

Non-Premier: $50 × 8 members = $400 − $100 remitted = $300 pot
Premier: $50 × 8 members = $400 + $100 received = $500 pot

Amendments

  • 8/8/2025 Commissioner vacancies (Premier or Non-Premier) open a 24-hour volunteer window; if none is resolved, the Supreme Commissioner appoints a successor. All decisions are final.
  • 8/7/2026 Nelson was unilaterally removed from the league, replaced by Chris Braunger. Any complaints from Nelson are directed to Nate, Conor, and Poundo.
